25 April 2026 · Past event

Our first meeting

Our first meeting was attended by 85 people representing 60 organisations. Workshop discussions revealed enthusiasm for sharing contacts, skills, knowledge and practical experience. There was a strong feeling that - while excellent work is already happening in many different contexts - we could achieve more by finding opportunities to collaborate.

7 November 2026 · Past event

Building nature corridors

A second, packed and lively session in November 2026 formed working groups around the potential nature corridors. Participants shared news from their projects, talked about how they could work together and how they might link up our green spaces for nature. An impressive number of practical steps forward were agreed. Watch this space for news of progress on these!
